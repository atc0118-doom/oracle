      ORACLE is an experimental, non-commercial dashboard built from public news/event feeds and rule-based heuristics, with optional AI-assisted phrasing. Scores, driver weights, and regional rankings are hand-tuned estimates, not a validated or peer-reviewed risk model. This platform provides situational awareness only — it is not a prediction system, not investment/policy/safety advice, and should not be relied on for decisions.
      
  
      

  
      
    
      
      
      
      HOW WE CALCULATE
      
      
      
      
      Scores combine public signals, source confidence, weighted risk drivers, and AI-assisted reasoning. ORACLE separates facts, assessment, and outlook for reliability.
